Personal Finance Manager — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

1. Who we are

This app is developed by Roni Riman. It is a local-first personal finance tool. There is no advertising, analytics, tracking server or backend database by default.

2. What data the app handles

  • Finance data: accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, notes, reminders and settings are stored on your device in IndexedDB. It is never uploaded automatically.
  • Profile: the name and photo you optionally enter are stored only on your device.
  • Google Account: if you sign in, we receive your Google name, email and profile picture. They are used only to identify your backup account inside the app.
  • Drive backups: only when you tap “Backup now” is an AES-256-GCM encrypted file written to the private appDataFolder of your own Google Drive. The passphrase is never stored.

3. Permissions used

  • Internet: only for Google sign-in and explicit Drive backup/restore.
  • Notifications: only for reminders you create. You can turn them off in Settings.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, share or transmit your personal or financial data to third parties. Google is used only when you explicitly choose sign-in or backup.

5. Retention and deletion

Finance data stays on your device until you delete it or reset the app. Google account details and Drive backups can be removed from Settings → Privacy & Account → Delete Google account data. Uninstalling the app removes local data on that device.

6. Security

Backups are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before upload. OAuth tokens are kept in memory only. No client secret is stored in this app.

7. Children

This app is not directed to children under 13.

8. Changes

Updated policies will be shown inside the app and on this page.
